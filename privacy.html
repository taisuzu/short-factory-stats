Privacy Policy

Japan Off Script — last updated 2026-07-28

Who this is for

This is a personal tool used by a single individual to publish their own short videos to their own social media accounts. It is not offered to other people and has no other users.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data from any third party. The tool has no sign-up, no user accounts, and no analytics on visitors.

Data we access

With the account owner's explicit authorization, the tool accesses their own social media accounts in order to:

  • upload videos the owner created, to the owner's own account
  • read public performance figures for those videos (view counts, watch time)

Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private repository secrets. They are never shared with anyone and are not transmitted to any third party other than the platform that issued them.

Published statistics

This site displays the owner's own video titles and view counts. No information about viewers or any other person is shown or stored.

Third-party services

The tool sends requests to YouTube (Google), TikTok, Anthropic, fal.ai, and Wikimedia Commons. Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Authorization can be revoked at any time by the account owner from the platform's own settings screen, which immediately ends all access.
